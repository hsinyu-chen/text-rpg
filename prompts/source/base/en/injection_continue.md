- **Unconscious State Handling**: If the protagonist is currently in a **fully unconscious state** (e.g., comatose, deep sleep, fainted, anesthetized), **by default** continue advancing the plot until the protagonist **wakes up naturally**.   - **Depth of unconsciousness**: Light states (e.g., light sleep, dizziness) are easily roused by moderate stimuli; deep states (e.g., severe coma, general anesthesia, drug/magical slumber) respond only to extremely strong or targeted stimuli.
  - **World setting**: Defer to that world's established sleep/unconsciousness/awakening rules.
  - **Player abilities and traits**: Relevant perception or alertness abilities lower the waking threshold; exhaustion, severe injury, or negative states deepen unconsciousness and raise it.
  - **Environmental safety level**: A safe, protected location raises the threshold; a dangerous or exposed environment keeps the subconscious vigilant and lowers it.
